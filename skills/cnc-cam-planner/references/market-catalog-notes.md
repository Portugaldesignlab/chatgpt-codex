# Market Reality Notes

This skill is calibrated around common vertical machining centers and mainstream carbide tooling families.

## Representative Machines

- Haas VF series (job-shop baseline, broad availability)
- SYIL X7 class (SME-friendly 3-axis CNC mills)
- DMG MORI CMX class (higher-spec VMC workflows)

## Representative Tooling

- Flat end mills (6-12 mm) for roughing/pocketing
- Ball nose end mills (4-6 mm) for 3D finishing
- Brand families commonly available: Sandvik, Kennametal, Mitsubishi, YG-1

## Materials in Scope

- 6061-T6 aluminum
- 1045 medium-carbon steel
- C360 brass
- Engineering plastics (e.g., POM/Delrin)
- Hardwoods (for prototyping workflows)

## Constraints

- Do not claim production-ready NC output without postprocessor and machine simulation validation.
- Treat holder geometry, stick-out, and fixture geometry as required inputs for collision confidence.
